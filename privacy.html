Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Noor-e-Raah Uploader (“the app”) handles data. The app is a private, non-commercial tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel “नूर-ए-राह” (Noor-e-Raah).

YouTube API Services

The app uses the YouTube API Services. By using the app you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What data the app accesses

  • The app reads and writes data only for our own YouTube channel: our videos (upload, title, description, thumbnail, privacy and publish time), our playlists, and comments on our own videos.
  • The app does not access, collect or store data of any other YouTube user or channel.

How data is stored and shared

  • The OAuth access/refresh token is stored only locally on the channel owner's own computer.
  • We do not share, sell, rent or transfer any data or tokens to anyone.
  • The app and this website use no cookies, no analytics and no tracking.

Removing access

Access granted to the app can be revoked at any time from your Google Account permissions page: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the local token file also removes the app's access from that computer.

Questions about this policy: nooreraahofficial@gmail.com
